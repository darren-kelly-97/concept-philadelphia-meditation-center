Sunday evening, Wednesday evening, and every Saturday morning, the hall opens and the cushions come out. Some sessions are also on Zoom. Check below for which is which, or call us if you are not sure.

    Every week

    Sunday, 6:30 PM
    Doors open at 6:30, sitting begins at 7:00. Half hour sitting, 15 minutes walking, half hour sitting, then tea.
    Wednesday, 7:00 PM
    Meditation and Dharma talk with Venerable Bhante Chandrawansa begins at 7:30, hybrid in person and on Zoom. Teachings from the Majjhima Nikaya.
    Saturday, 9:30 AM
    Open through Noon. Come for all of it or part of it. Sit, walk, break for tea, and repeat, ending by Noon.
    4th Sunday, 11:00 AM
    A short Dharmette, a sit, and a practice discussion, until 12:30. Also on Zoom.

    A donation-based nonprofit, run by volunteers

    PMC is a 501(c)(3) public charity, registered in Pennsylvania since 1998. There is no fee to sit with us. The center runs on dana, the traditional practice of freewill giving, and on a small Supporting Membership for those who want to give regularly.
