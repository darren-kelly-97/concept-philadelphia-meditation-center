Weekly sessions by day

6 AMNoon10 PM

  1. SundayEvery week

    7:00 PMSunday Evening MeditationCenter opens 6:30 PM, sitting begins 7:00 PM. Half hour sitting, 15 minutes walking meditation, second half hour sitting, then tea. In person and on Zoom.

  2. WednesdayEvery week

    7:30 PMWednesday Meditation and Dharma TalkHybrid session led by Venerable Bhante Chandrawansa. Talks draw on the Majjhima Nikaya, the Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha.

    7:30 PMMeditation and RecoveryDoors open 7:00 PM, meeting starts 7:30 PM. Also on Zoom. Contact Greg Ardire with questions, through the Center by email or at (610) 853-8200.

  3. SaturdayEvery week

    9:30 AMSaturday Meditation Sessions9:30 AM to Noon. Sit 30 minutes, walk 15, break for tea, repeat at 10:30, final sit around 11:30. Stay for all of it or leave whenever suits you. In person.

  4. 4th SundayOf each month

    11:00 AM4th Sunday Dharmette, Sit and Practice Discussion11:00 AM to 12:30 PM ET. A short talk, a sit, and a discussion touching on Emptiness, the Brahma Viharas, and the Paramitas. Also on Zoom.
